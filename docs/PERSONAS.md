# Personas

## Primary: Field Hunter / Flipper

Works estate sales, thrift, garage, storage, online lots. Needs decisions in under a minute while standing in a crowded room or scrolling a feed. Values honesty over hype because wrong buys destroy margins.

## Secondary: Desk Reseller

Uses the same tool after the fact to generate listings and track what actually sold vs predicted.

Build mobile field experience first; desk experience second.
